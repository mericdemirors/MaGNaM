Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 22, 2026

Your privacy is critically important to us. At MaGNaM, we have a few fundamental principles:

  • We are thoughtful about the personal information we ask you to provide and the personal information that we collect about you through the operation of our services.
  • We store personal information for only as long as we have a reason to keep it.
  • We aim to make it as simple as possible for you to control what information is shared publicly (or kept private), indexed by search engines, and permanently deleted.
  • We help protect you from overreaching government demands for your personal information.
  • We aim for full transparency on how we gather, use, and share your personal information.

Below is our Privacy Policy, which incorporates and clarifies these principles.

Information We Collect

We only collect information about you if we have a reason to do so — for example, to provide our Services, to communicate with you, to maintain account security, or to make our Services better.

We collect this information from three sources: if and when you provide information to us, automatically through operating our Services, and from outside sources. Let's go over the information that we collect.

Information You Provide to Us

It's probably no surprise that we collect information that you provide to us directly. Here are some examples:

  • Basic account information: We ask for basic information from you in order to set up your account. For example, we require individuals who sign up for a MaGNaM account to provide an email address.
  • Public profile information: If you have an account with us, we collect the information that you provide for your public profile. For example, if you have a MaGNaM account, your username is part of that public profile, along with any other information you put into your public profile, like a photo. Your public profile information is just that — public — so please keep that in mind when deciding what information you would like to include.
  • Payment and contact information: Currently there is no payment related data we collect. The payment-like aspect of MaGNaM is a profile's wallet, which is just a number that lives in the app and (at this time) has no other connection to an external payment service.
  • Content information: You might provide us with information about you in draft and published content, including portfolio information, manga, competition submissions, comments, or other text, images, documents, and files that you upload or publish through the Services.
  • Communications with us: You may also provide us with information when you communicate with us about a support question, complaint, report, or other request.

Information We Collect Automatically

We also collect some information automatically:

  • Log information: Like most online service providers, we collect information that web browsers and servers typically make available, including the browser type, IP address, referring site, the date and time of access, and operating system. We collect log information when you use our Services — for example, when you create or make changes to your MaGNaM profile.
  • Transactional information: Currently there is no payment related data we collect.
  • Usage information: We collect information about your usage of our Services, such as actions needed to provide, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Services.
  • Information from cookies and sessions: A cookie is a string of information that a website stores on a visitor's computer, and that the visitor's browser provides to the website each time the visitor returns. We use cookies and similar technologies that are necessary for account authentication, sessions, security, and access preferences for our Services.

Information We Collect from Other Sources

We may also get information about you from other sources. For example:

  • Google Sign-In: If you create or log in to your MaGNaM account through Google, we may receive Google account information made available through the authentication flow, including your email address and whether that email address has been verified by Google. We use this information to authenticate you, create or connect your MaGNaM account, and maintain your account.

Google Sign-In in more detail

When you register or log in using Google, we only request the absolute minimum information needed to create and manage your account: your email address and whether Google has verified it. We do not access your contacts, real name, or profile picture (our system automatically generates a default name and avatar for you).

We use this information strictly to allow you to log in, secure your account, and send necessary account notifications. Your email address and account data are stored securely on our private database (hosted on a Hetzner server) and are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

You remain in complete control of your data. Unverified accounts are automatically cleared from our system after a set period, and you can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time through your profile settings.

Account Security & Authentication: When you register using a standard email, you choose your own password and must verify your account via a secure link sent to your inbox. If you register via Google, we automatically generate a secure password for you and instantly verify your account (if Google has already verified your email). To protect you from imitation attacks, if someone creates an unverified account using your email address and you later log in with Google, we will automatically secure the account by resetting the password and notifying you. If you ever change your email address in your profile settings, your account will reset to an unverified state to confirm you own the new address, but we will not change your password so you don't get locked out. For your own safety, we strongly recommend using a private, unmonitored email address, keeping your passwords secret, and verifying your account promptly to prevent it from being automatically deleted.

The information we receive depends on the authentication services you use and what information is made available through the relevant authentication flow.

User-published content is retained while it remains published or otherwise stored in the Services, and deletion/removal may take additional time from backups or cached copies.
