Stage 2 — Identify the legal basis for each category
For each type of processing, we'll determine the appropriate GDPR legal basis.
The GDPR requires the privacy information to explain things including the controller's identity/contact information, purposes of processing and legal basis.
Typical possibilities are:
Contract
For things necessary to provide the MaGNaM account/service.
For example:
Creating and maintaining your MaGNaM account.
Legal obligation
Only where you actually have a legal obligation to retain/process something.
Legitimate interests
Potentially:
security
fraud prevention
abuse prevention
protecting the service
technical logging
But we should not automatically label everything "legitimate interest."
Consent
For things where consent is genuinely the appropriate basis, such as certain optional communications/cookies.
We'll decide this feature by feature.

Stage 4 — Determine retention periods
This is one of the areas where people make bad generic policies.
Don't write:
"We retain your data as long as necessary."
for everything.
We'll determine actual periods.
For example, your existing code already has a concrete retention mechanism for unverified accounts:
non_verified_since -> countdown -> automatic deletion
We'll document the actual period you configured.
For other things we'll establish sensible policies, such as:
account data → until account deletion
uploaded content → until deletion/removal
security logs → X days
transactional records → X years where legally required
abandoned/unverified accounts → your configured period
And importantly, the policy should match what your software actually does.
